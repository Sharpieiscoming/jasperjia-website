# Kouzan Brush Font Gyousyo / 衡山毛筆フォント行書

Calligraphy and font by Kouzan Aoyagi (青柳衡山). Original font copyright/name record: `KouzanAoyagi`.

Official distributor: Musashi System, <https://opentype.jp/kouzangyousho.htm>
Original download: <https://opentype.jp/bin/KouzanGyousho.zip>

The official distribution page permits free use and unrestricted commercial use, including logos and redistribution in publications/CDs, and provides no warranty. Its published permission statement reads:

> 無料でご利用できます。
> 商用利用可能で制限はありません。
> ロゴ等へも自由にご利用いただけます。

This font is not represented as SIL OFL. The website's `jasper-motto.woff2` is a locally hosted WOFF2 subset of the original font for the Chinese motto only. Original outlines and attribution are preserved. No artificial slant is applied. The permission above also allows use as an embedded webfont; a public webfont conversion is documented at <https://leafscape.be/fonts/36.html>.
